# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 6, 2026.** This policy covers the CBPay mobile
apps (iOS and Android) and the CBPay platform behind them.

## 1. Data we collect

- **Account data:** name, email, country, account type, verification
  documents (KYC/KYB) required by anti-money-laundering law.
- **Financial data:** balances, transactions, beneficiaries, cards and
  statements you create or view in the app.
- **Device data:** app version, device model, OS version, push token and
  a random device identifier. Used for security and fraud prevention.
- **Support data:** messages and attachments you send to support.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos or files outside
what you explicitly upload (e.g. verification documents).

## 2. How we use it

- Operate your account: payments, collections, conversions, cards.
- Verify identity and comply with AML/CFT_CFT_TMP obligations.
- Prevent fraud and abuse; secure sessions and devices.
- Send transactional notifications you enable (push/email).
- Improve the app with aggregated, de-identified analytics.

## 3. Sharing

We do **not** sell your data and we do **not** share it with advertisers.
Data is shared only with:

- Payment and banking rails needed to execute your operations.
- Identity-verification and screening providers (KYC/KYB, AML/CFT).
- Authorities when the law requires it.

Our analytics run on our own infrastructure; the apps ship **no
third-party advertising or tracking SDKs**.

## 4. Security

- TLS 1.2+ with certificate pinning on every API call.
- Tokens stored in the iOS Keychain / Android EncryptedSharedPreferences.
- Biometric lock and second-factor confirmation on money movements.
- Automatic session revocation on compromised (rooted/jailbroken) devices.

## 5. Retention and your rights

We keep data while your account is active and as long as financial
regulation requires afterwards. You can request access, correction,
portability or deletion at **privacy@cbpayapp.com**. Deletion is subject
to legal retention duties; we always confirm what was deleted.

## 6. Contact

Questions about this policy: **privacy@cbpayapp.com**. Material changes
are announced in-app at least 15 days before they take effect.
