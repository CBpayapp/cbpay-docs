# CBPay API documentation

Move money across Latin America: payouts, payins, cards, crypto and banking — one API, six balances.

## Start integrating

- **Quickstart** - Your first API call in five minutes: keys, sandbox, and a test payout.
- **Authentication** - API keys, scopes, and how requests are signed.
- **Sandbox & testing** - Test mode, magic values, and simulated rails.
- **Money model** - Six independent balances and how money moves between them.
## Build products

- **Fiat payouts** - Send money to local bank accounts across the region.
- **Fiat payins** - Collect in local currency and get credited automatically.
- **Checkout** - One payment link; your payer picks country, method or crypto.
- **Cards** - Issue cards that spend from any balance in real time.
- **On-chain crypto** - Fund and withdraw over TRON, Ethereum and Bitcoin.
- **Swaps** - Convert between your balances at your account's rate.
- **Internal transfers** - Move balance to any CBPay account, instantly and free.
- **Banking** - Real bank accounts in your name on international rails.
## Reconcile

- **Statements** - Account statements in JSON, PDF and Excel.
- **Receipts** - Signed, verifiable receipts for every operation.
- **Public tracking** - Shareable live status pages for your transactions.
- **Analytics** - Volumes, counts and dimensions for your dashboards.
## Operate & reference

- **Webhooks** - Events pushed to your systems, signed and replayable.
- **Real-time events** - Subscribe to the live event stream.
- **Errors** - Every error code and how to fix it.
- **FAQ** - Answers to the questions integrators ask most.
- **Postman** - Ready-made collections for every surface.
- **Docs MCP** - Let AI assistants query these docs for you.
- **Service status** - Live health of every corridor and rail.
